Dog Bed Buying Guide: Orthopedic Foam, Sizing, and What to Actually Buy

Updated July 26, 2026 · WhatAreBest.com Editorial Team

Most dog beds flatten within months. Here is how to find one that actually lasts and supports your dog properly.

The Foam Quality Problem

The dog bed industry is dominated by cheap polyester fiberfill and low-density foam that compresses flat within 3–6 months. Your dog ends up sleeping on a fabric shell. The solution is high-density memory foam or certified orthopedic foam — the same standards used in human mattresses. Only a handful of brands meet this benchmark; Big Barker is the only one with published clinical trial data.

When Dogs Need Orthopedic Support

All large breeds over 60 lbs benefit from orthopedic support to protect developing and aging joints. It becomes essential for: dogs over 7 years old, breeds prone to hip dysplasia (Labs, German Shepherds, Golden Retrievers, Rottweilers), any dog recovering from surgery or diagnosed with arthritis, and dogs that spend more than 16 hours a day sleeping (which is most of them).

Sizing Your Dog's Bed

Measure your dog from nose to base of tail when fully stretched out on their side. Add 6–12 inches to that measurement. This is your minimum bed length. For dogs that curl tightly, measure the circle diameter of their curl and add 6 inches. When uncertain between sizes, choose larger — dogs consistently use the full surface when it's available.

Waterproof Liner vs. Waterproof Cover

A waterproof liner inside the cover protects the foam from accidents, drool, and wet paws indefinitely. A waterproof cover alone provides surface protection but allows moisture to eventually reach the foam at seams and zippers. For puppies, senior dogs with incontinence, or any dog with allergies, the liner is essential for bed longevity.

Machine Washable: The Practical Requirement

A dog bed cover you can't machine wash is a dog bed you will eventually throw away. Verify the cover zips completely off, fits a standard home washing machine, and can handle hot-water cycles. The foam itself typically spot-cleans; the cover takes the daily wear.
